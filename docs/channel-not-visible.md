# Channel not visible

## Customers may say
- "I cannot see Asianet Movies."
- "Some channels are missing."
- "My TV says the channel is not subscribed."

## What it means
The TV works and some channels show, but one or more channels are missing. If nothing shows on the TV at all, use the no signal steps instead.

## Ask the customer first
Ask: "What message do you see on the TV screen?" Then match the message:
- Error 3: "You are not subscribed to this channel."
- Error 5: "Your account is deactivated, kindly recheck."

## Steps for error 3 and error 5
Do not take a callback request for these two errors. Raise an IT ticket instead.
1. Check that the connection is active and not suspended. If it is suspended, explain the suspension. Do not refresh.
2. Check that the missing channel is in one of the customer's packs. If it is not, tell the customer which pack has it and offer to add it (see the add and drop packs document). A refresh will not fix this.
3. Do a refresh from the server. Ask the customer to keep the set-top box switched on and check the channel after a few minutes.
4. If the channel is still missing, do a hard refresh from the server. Ask the customer to check again.
5. If the channel is still missing after both refreshes, raise a ticket to the IT team.
6. Tell the customer the ticket number, and that the IT team will check the server and fix it within 2 hours.

## About the IT ticket
- The IT team checks the problem on the server.
- The turnaround time is 2 hours.
- Always give the customer the ticket ID.
- Do not create a technician work order for this problem. A technician visit does not fix it.

## Other messages
If the customer sees a different message, or no message, ask for the exact words and take a callback request (see the escalation document).

## Do not
- Do not take a callback request for error 3 or error 5. Use the IT ticket instead.
- Do not create a work order for a missing channel.
- Do not add a pack without the customer's confirmation.
- Do not change packs on a suspended connection.

## What to tell the customer
- Explain what you are doing in simple words, for example "I am refreshing your account" and "I am doing a hard refresh".
- After raising the ticket: "I have raised ticket number [ticket ID] with our IT team. They will check it from the server, and your channel should work within 2 hours."
- Ask for confirmation before adding a pack.
