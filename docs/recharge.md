# Recharge

## Customers may say
"How much should I recharge?", "What is the minimum recharge?", "My balance is low".

## Rules
- The minimum recharge amount on our platform is 200 rupees. Every recharge must be at least 200 rupees.
- If the customer only needs a small amount (for example, 72 rupees are missing), they still have to recharge at least 200 rupees.
- If the missing amount is more than 200 rupees, the customer must recharge at least the missing amount.
- To resume a suspended connection, the balance only needs to cover one day of burn rate. The customer does not need to recharge the monthly amount.

## What to tell the customer
- Say that the minimum recharge is 200 rupees.
- If the customer wants to add a pack, explain how much is missing and that the recharge is at least 200 rupees.
