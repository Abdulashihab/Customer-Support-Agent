# Temporary suspension

## Customers may say
"Pause my connection", "I am going out of town", "stop my TV for some days".

## What we need from the customer
1. The reason for the suspension.
2. The start date and the end date.

## Rules
- The suspension must start tomorrow or later. The day runs from 12 midnight to 12 midnight, so today cannot be used.
- The suspension must be at least 5 days long. The start date and the end date both count as days.
- The balance must cover at least one day of burn rate. Burn rate per day is the monthly charge divided by 30.
- Only one suspension request can be open at a time.
- A connection that is already suspended cannot be suspended again.
- The connection stays active until the start date.

## Resuming a suspended connection
- The set-top box must be switched on while the connection is resumed.
- The balance only needs to cover one day of burn rate. It does not need to cover the full monthly charge.
- Example: if the burn rate is 14 rupees per day and the balance is 15 rupees, the connection can be resumed. The TV will run for about one day.
- A balance equal to one day of burn rate is also enough.
- The customer does not need to recharge the monthly amount.
- If the balance is less than one day of burn rate, the customer must recharge at least 200 rupees before the connection can be resumed.

## What to tell the customer
- If the period is too short, explain the 5-day minimum and offer a longer period.
- If the start date is today, offer tomorrow as the earliest start.
- If the balance is too low for the suspension, ask the customer to recharge first. The minimum recharge is 200 rupees.
- Before resuming, ask the customer to switch on the set-top box.
- If the balance is too low to resume, tell the customer to recharge a minimum of 200 rupees. The monthly amount is not needed.
