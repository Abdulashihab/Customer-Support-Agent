# Adding and dropping packs

## Customers may say
"Add sports channels", "remove the kids pack", "my bill is too high", "I want to change my pack".

## Adding a pack
- The balance must cover the new monthly charge. That is the current charge plus the price of the new pack. If not, the customer must recharge first. The recharge must be at least the missing amount, and never less than 200 rupees.
- A pack the customer already has cannot be added again.
- A suspended connection cannot change packs.
- If some channels of the new pack are already in the customer's other packs, the customer still pays the full price of the new pack. There is no discount for overlapping channels. Tell the customer before adding.

## Dropping a pack
- The base pack (DSK-BASIC) is mandatory and can never be dropped.
- Only a pack the customer actually has can be dropped.
- A suspended connection cannot change packs.
- If another pack of the customer also gives a channel, the customer keeps that channel after dropping.
- The monthly charge goes down by the price of the dropped pack.

## What to tell the customer
- Before adding or dropping, say which channels they will get or lose.
- Ask the customer to confirm before making the change.
